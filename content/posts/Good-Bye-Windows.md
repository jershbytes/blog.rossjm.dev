# Introduction 

**Welcome Everyone**, This blog post has been a long time coming. I sat for the last two years trying to think what I should write about. The best thing that came to mind was let me tell the people why I left windows and went full linux.

## The Why
As a long time windows user and someone who had been using it mostly from 1998 to about 2025. I used it exclusively during that time even when I had started learning more Linux it still was my fall back when things got tough. But that had changed in the last couple years as I had gotten better in my career and in my skills. But that's now why I left that would too boring and predictable. The real reason was the sloppiness of microsoft and what had poured into Windows. First it was an update here and there and some AI integration there which was not bad at all, but made little sense to me. I'd get on to game and the computer would have another patch from something upstream that broke and was easily preventable had Microsoft not gotten into bed with OpenAI. The nail in the coffin for me that made me think , I should really leave?! [Co-pilot in Explorer], I thought why in the hell does this need to be here at all. So at that point I decided to move the main Rig over to Linux.


## I use arch btw
So you're probably wondering from that header.Oh you went full vanilla arch.At first that was the answer I downloaded and ISO and used the [archinstall] script. That went great I was coding and gaming just fine but felt there was more and really wanted to search for something. But then it hit me [my bluesky post]. Someone had mentioned [CachyOS]. So I gave it a shot and boy color me impressed. It was fast, easy and simple to use.
